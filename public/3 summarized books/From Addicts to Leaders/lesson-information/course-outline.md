# From Addicts to Leaders

## Guidance Book Summary

`From Addicts to Leaders` is a guidance book built from the manuscript by Craig Petersen. It is not a full My Mentor course. Its focus is addiction recovery, leadership, character, purpose, family restoration, and constructive social contribution.

The guidance book should follow a progression from:

`brokenness -> inner change -> responsibility -> leadership -> restoration`

## Suggested Reading Structure

- `3 chapters`
- `21 lessons total`
- `6-12 minutes` per lesson depending on block depth
- Suggested reading rhythm:
  `welcome -> reading -> mentor note/quote -> pause & reflect -> journal -> mood check-in -> action -> reflection check -> complete`

## Chapter 1: Leadership and Recovery

### Chapter Overview

This chapter introduces the book's central claim: recovery must grow beyond stopping destructive behavior and into responsible, character-based leadership. It defines leadership as service, truthfulness, maturity, and influence rooted in care for others.

### Lessons

- `Lesson 1: Why Recovery Needs Leadership`
- `Lesson 2: Leadership as Character, Not Status`
- `Lesson 3: Service, Responsibility, and Influence`
- `Lesson 4: What a True Leader Is`
- `Lesson 5: What a True Leader Is Not`
- `Lesson 6: Stewardship and Trust`
- `Lesson 7: Growing Into Mature Leadership`

## Chapter 2: The Individual and Inner Change

### Chapter Overview

This chapter focuses on the individual person in recovery. It explores identity, purpose, personal development, addictive behavior, and the need for deep inner transformation rather than surface-level change.

### Lessons

- `Lesson 1: Seeing the Whole Person`
- `Lesson 2: Character Traits for a Better Life`
- `Lesson 3: Discovering Purpose`
- `Lesson 4: Gifts, Growth, and Personal Development`
- `Lesson 5: Understanding Addictive Behavior`
- `Lesson 6: Why Stopping Use Is Not Enough`
- `Lesson 7: Renewing Lifestyle, Habits, and Responsibility`

## Chapter 3: Family, Restoration, and Social Renewal

### Chapter Overview

This chapter widens the lens from the individual to the family and society. It connects addiction to family pain, household instability, weak role modeling, and wider social harm, then points toward restoration through truth, responsibility, care, and leadership.

### Lessons

- `Lesson 1: Why Family Matters in Recovery`
- `Lesson 2: Addiction and Family Breakdown`
- `Lesson 3: Responsibility in the Home`
- `Lesson 4: Parent Roles, Care, and Stability`
- `Lesson 5: Rebuilding Trust and Relationships`
- `Lesson 6: Leadership for Stronger Communities`
- `Lesson 7: From Recovery to Social Renewal`

## Implementation Notes

- Keep the tone practical, steady, and dignifying rather than academic.
- Avoid making this feel like a duplicate of `Uprooting Drug Abuse`; it is a follow-on guidance book about identity, leadership, and restoration.
- Use examples around responsibility, service, family repair, and trustworthy living.
- The strongest recurring action theme is:
  `becoming someone who can lead self well, serve others well, and rebuild trust over time`
