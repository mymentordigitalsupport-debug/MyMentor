# Member Progress Tracking

## Verification

Yes. Member progress tracking is already implemented in the member dashboard.

Members can currently see:

- Overall course-completion percentage.
- Completed lessons against total lessons.
- Completed chapters against total chapters.
- The next chapter and next lesson.
- Lessons completed in the last seven days.
- Learning time based on lesson-step activity.
- Chapter-by-chapter completion.
- Journal-entry count.
- Mood-check-in count and latest mood.
- Saved-insight count.
- Quiz accuracy, where quiz answers exist.
- Recent activity for lessons, reflections, mood check-ins, and saved insights.
- Achievement and milestone indicators.

This information is shown on the member Progress page and feeds into the member course/dashboard experience.

## Intended Member Experience

Progress tracking should feel encouraging, private, and practical. It should show members that small actions are building momentum, without making recovery feel like a competition.

The member should be able to answer four simple questions at any time:

- Where am I in my current journey?
- What should I do next?
- What have I already done?
- How am I doing emotionally and reflectively?

## Core Progress Journey

### 1. Start a journey

When a member selects a course, the platform creates their course-progress record.

The dashboard should show:

- Selected journey name.
- Current guidance path: Christian or religious.
- First available lesson.
- A clear “continue” action.

### 2. Work through lessons

As a member moves through lessons, the platform records lesson activity and lesson completion.

The dashboard should show:

- Current lesson.
- Next lesson.
- Lessons completed.
- Percentage complete.
- Chapter completion.

### 3. Reflect and check in

Reflections are part of progress, not separate from it.

The dashboard should include:

- Journal entries written.
- Mood check-ins completed.
- Latest mood recorded.
- Saved insights.
- Quiz understanding where applicable.

### 4. Recognise milestones

Milestones help members see progress that can otherwise feel invisible.

Current milestone ideas include:

- Journey started.
- First lesson completed.
- First reflection saved.
- First journal entry.
- First mood check-in.
- First chapter completed.
- Course completed.

## Streak Tracking

Streak tracking is not implemented yet.

When added, it should reward steady engagement without punishing a member for taking a break. A missed day should not make the member feel they have failed.

Recommended approach:

- Track active days: a completed lesson, journal entry, mood check-in, or saved reflection counts as meaningful engagement.
- Show a gentle “current rhythm” instead of an aggressive daily streak.
- Keep the longest positive rhythm as a private personal record.
- Use supportive language after gaps, such as “Welcome back. Your journey continues.”
- Do not reset or shame the member for missed days.

## Future Audio Contribution

Once audio is implemented, progress can also include:

- Audio lessons played.
- Audio practices completed.
- Listening time.
- Audio reflections saved.

Audio should count toward meaningful engagement and the member’s rhythm where appropriate.

## Privacy

All progress, journals, moods, and saved insights are private to the member unless a future feature explicitly adds consent-based sharing.
