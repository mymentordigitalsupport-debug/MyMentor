## Full course: Uprooting Drug Abuse

### Uprooting Drug Abuse - Christian Guidance

#### Chapter 1: The Stage of Creation

Chapter 1 lays the biblical foundation for the Christian-guided recovery journey. It begins with God's original design for humanity, then traces how identity, purpose, freedom, temptation, disobedience, consequences, stewardship, and heart-level pain all connect to the roots of addiction.

The goal of this chapter is to help the learner understand that addiction is not their identity, that God's design was good, and that lasting healing requires truth, responsibility, and restoration at the heart level.

- Lessons: 10

#### Chapter 2: The Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil

Chapter 2 examines how brokenness entered human experience and how it continues shaping desire, behavior, identity, and addiction. It moves from the meaning of the forbidden tree into the entrance of sin, the corruption of human nature, spiritual death, inherited brokenness, addictive patterns, destructive fruit, and God's restoring answer.

The goal of this chapter is to help the learner understand that drug abuse and addiction is connected to deeper spiritual and human realities, and that restoration becomes possible when lies are exposed, the bad fruit is faced truthfully, and God's way back is embraced.

- Lessons: 8

#### Chapter 3: The Concept of Abuse

Chapter 3 defines abuse more precisely and shows how it appears not only in substances, but also in thoughts, motives, relationships, coping patterns, and the inner life. It traces the line from misuse into abuse, exposes the abusive nature of sin, examines self-destruction, hidden heart issues, thought patterns, strongholds, and personal responsibility.

The goal of this chapter is to help the learner stop minimizing harm, recognize where abusive patterns have taken hold, and embrace responsibility as part of Christ-centered change and recovery.

- Lessons: 8

#### Chapter 4: The Real Reason People Do Drugs

Chapter 4 looks beneath drug abuse itself and examines the deeper reasons people reach for destructive substances. It explores escape, pain, trauma, spiritual emptiness, belonging, false comfort, deception, and the deeper needs of the heart that keep driving repeated use.

The goal of this chapter is to help the learner stop reducing addiction to the substance alone and begin understanding the real needs, wounds, lies, and longings that have been seeking relief through it.

- Lessons: 8

#### Chapter 5: The Stages of Drug Abuse

Chapter 5 traces how drug abuse progresses over time from curiosity and experimentation into repeated use, dependency, escalation, loss of control, financial loss, and the need for honest self-recognition. It ends by turning the learner toward God's way out.

The goal of this chapter is to help the learner understand drug abuse and addiction as a progressive journey, recognize their present stage with clarity, and begin responding to the pattern with seriousness, humility, and hope.

- Lessons: 9

#### Chapter 6: Spiritual Eyes of the Evil Nature

Chapter 6 focuses on spiritual awareness, distorted perception, deception, shame, fear, condemnation, blindness, and the restoration of sight through God's truth. It helps the learner understand how addiction shapes the way reality is interpreted and how truth can reorient that inner lens.

The goal of this chapter is to help the learner become more spiritually aware, more honest about distortion and accusation, and more grounded in God's truth as the lens for recovery.

- Lessons: 7

#### Chapter 7: The Two Laws

Chapter 7 explains the inner conflict between the law of sin and death and the law of the Spirit of life. It helps the learner interpret the struggle more accurately, distinguish flesh from Spirit, understand the weight of daily choices, and walk in obedience under grace.

The goal of this chapter is to give the learner a clearer framework for understanding why conflict exists, how obedience becomes practical, and how God's grace sustains recovery without compromise.

- Lessons: 7

#### Chapter 8: The Spiritual War

Chapter 8 helps the learner understand the spiritual war around drug abuse and addiction recovery. It focuses on warfare, the enemy's strategies, temptation and resistance, strongholds and bondage, the armor of God, victory through Christ, and standing firm in recovery.

The goal of this chapter is to make the learner more spiritually alert, better equipped, and steadier under pressure as they continue the recovery journey.

- Lessons: 7

#### Chapter 9: The Mind Battlefield

Chapter 9 focuses on the inner battle of thought processes, interpretation, emotions, and mental renewal. It guides the person through renewing the mind, naming harmful patterns, taking thoughts captive, answering cravings with truth, replacing lies with Scripture, building godly thinking, emotional maturity, and long-term stability.

The goal of this chapter is to help the person become more disciplined, truthful, emotionally steady, and mentally renewed in a way that supports lasting recovery.

- Lessons: 8

#### Chapter 10: The Tree of Life

Chapter 10 turns the person toward restored life in Christ. It focuses on Christ as the source of life, returning to God's design, the gift of salvation, receiving grace, spiritual growth, fruitful living, and walking in newness of life.

The goal of this chapter is to help the person move beyond defensive recovery into a life that is rooted in Christ, shaped by grace, and visible through growth and fruit.

- Lessons: 7

#### Chapter 11: Love

Chapter 11 closes the course by focusing on love as the foundational spiritual fruit for healing. It covers God's love, receiving acceptance, healthy self-understanding, forgiveness, loving others well, restoring relationships, compassion, and walking in love daily.

The goal of this chapter is to help the learner move from survival and restoration into a daily life shaped by love, truth, mercy, and purpose driven relationships.

- Lessons: 8

### Uprooting Drug Abuse - Religious Guidance

#### Chapter 1: The Stage of Creation

Chapter 1 lays the foundation for your recovery journey.

It begins by exploring the original purpose and design for humanity, then traces how identity, purpose, freedom, temptation, choices, consequences, stewardship, and heart-level pain all connect to the roots of addiction.

The goal of this chapter is to help you understand that addiction is not your identity, that every person was created with inherent worth and purpose, and that lasting healing requires truth, responsibility, spiritual growth, and restoration of the heart.

- Lessons: 10

#### Chapter 2: The Tree of Knowledge of Good and Evil

Chapter 2 explores how brokenness became part of the human experience and how it continues to influence our desires, choices, identity, and addictions. It examines the meaning of the forbidden tree as a symbol, also as symbolic truth and real physical chemical potency. The progression from temptation to destructive choices, the impact of brokenness on human nature, spiritual disconnection, inherited patterns, addictive cycles, the consequences of our actions, and the journey toward restoration.

The goal of this chapter is to help you understand that addiction is connected to deeper emotional, spiritual, and human realities. Lasting recovery becomes possible when we recognize unhealthy beliefs, face the consequences of our choices with honesty, and embrace the path of healing, restoration, and renewed purpose offered through our faith.

- Lessons: 8

#### Chapter 3: The Concept of Abuse

Chapter 3 explores the meaning of abuse and demonstrates how it can appear not only through substances, but also through thoughts, attitudes, motives, relationships, coping strategies, and the inner life. It examines the progression from healthy use to misuse, the destructive nature of harmful choices, self-destructive behaviors, hidden struggles of the heart, unhealthy thought patterns, deeply rooted strongholds, and the importance of taking responsibility for lasting change.

The goal of this chapter is to help you recognize harmful patterns without minimizing them, understand how abuse affects every area of life, and embrace personal responsibility as an essential part of faith-centered healing, growth, and recovery.

- Lessons: 8

#### Chapter 4: The Real Reason People Do Drugs

Chapter 4 explores the deeper reasons people turn to drugs by looking beyond the substance itself. It examines the search for escape from so-called reality, dealing with emotional pain, trauma, spiritual emptiness, the need to belong, false sources of comfort, unhealthy beliefs, and the deeper needs of the heart that often drive repeated substance abuse.

The goal of this chapter is to help you understand that drug abuse and addiction doesn't mean it's the source of life's problems. Lasting recovery begins when you honestly identify the deeper wounds, unmet needs, unhealthy thinking, and spiritual struggles that have been seeking relief. Healing becomes possible when those needs are addressed through truth, building healthy relationships, personal growth, and the guidance of your faith.

- Lessons: 8

#### Chapter 5: The Three Stages of Drug Abuse

Chapter 5 explores how drug addiction develops over time rather than appearing all at once. It follows the progression from curiosity and experimentation to repeated use, dependency, strong desire, loss of control, growing personal cost, honest self-recognition, and ultimately the path toward lasting freedom and recovery.

The goal of this chapter is to help you understand addiction as a gradual process, recognize your current stage with honesty, and respond with wisdom, humility, responsibility, and hope. By understanding how addiction grows, you become better equipped to break destructive patterns before they become even more deeply rooted.

- Lessons: 9

#### Chapter 6: Perception, Deception, Shame, and Spiritual Blindness

Chapter 6 explores how addiction affects the way people think, interpret reality, and respond to life. It examines spiritual awareness, distorted perception, deception, shame, fear, guilt, condemnation, spiritual blindness, and the restoration of clear thinking through the wisdom and truth found within one's faith.

The goal of this chapter is to help you recognize how drug abuse and addiction can distort your perspective, identify unhealthy beliefs that keep destructive addiction alive, and develop greater spiritual awareness. As you learn to replace distorted thinking with truth, honesty, and the guidance of your faith, you build a stronger foundation for complete healing and recovery.

- Lessons: 7

#### Chapter 7: The Two Ways of Living

Chapter 7 explores the ongoing inner conflict between destructive patterns and the path that leads to healing, freedom, lasting growth and a fulfilling inner life. It helps you understand why this struggle exists, how your daily choices shape your future, and how living according to the values of your faith strengthens recovery.

The goal of this chapter is to give you a practical framework for understanding inner conflict, making wise decisions, and building a life guided by truth, responsibility, and spiritual growth. As you learn to recognize unhealthy patterns and intentionally choose healthier ones, you develop greater freedom, resilience, and hope.

- Lessons: 7

#### Chapter 8: The Spiritual War

Chapter 8 helps the learner understand the spiritual battle surrounding addiction recovery. It explores spiritual warfare, the enemy's strategies, temptation and resistance, strongholds and bondage, the armor of God, victory through Christ, and standing firm throughout the recovery journey.

The goal of this chapter is to help the learner become spiritually alert, better equipped, and steadfast under pressure while continuing to grow in freedom through Christ. As learners develop discernment, strengthen their dependence on Christ, and stand firmly in biblical truth, they build a stronger foundation for lifelong recovery.

- Lessons: 7

#### Chapter 9: The Mind Battlefield

Chapter 9 focuses on the internal battle of thoughts, interpretation, emotions, and mental discipline during recovery. It shifts the focus from external behavior to the inner world, where many decisions are formed long before actions take place.

The goal of this chapter is to help you become mentally disciplined, emotionally steady, and grounded in truth so that your recovery is not constantly destabilized by unhealthy thinking patterns. As you learn to renew your mind, challenge harmful thoughts, and build healthier mental habits, you create a stronger foundation for lasting freedom.

- Lessons: 8

#### Chapter 10: The Tree of Life

Chapter 10 shifts the focus from simply overcoming addiction to building a life that is deeply rooted in spiritual growth, healing, and purpose. Rather than centering on one specific faith tradition, this chapter explores how drawing closer to the Divine, living according to the values of your faith, and developing a healthy spiritual foundation can support lasting recovery.

The goal of this chapter is to help you move beyond defensive recovery into a life rooted in faith, shaped by grace, strengthened through personal growth, and reflected in the way you live each day.

- Lessons: 7
