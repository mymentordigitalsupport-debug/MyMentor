# My Mentor

_Last generated: 2026-10-26. Public platform knowledge only._

## What My Mentor is

My Mentor is a guided growth platform built around books, structured courses, chapters, lessons, reflection, journaling, and daily action. It helps people move from survival into purpose through clear guidance, steady structure, and honest support.

## Core approach

- Truth: clear and direct without shaming.
- Structure: a steady sequence of courses, chapters, lessons, and actions.
- Care: humane, honest, and safe support.

## What members can do

- Explore published articles and reflections.
- Register and sign in to a member dashboard.
- Follow published courses and lessons at their own pace.
- Record private journal entries, mood check-ins, and lesson progress in their account.
- Apply to become an author and submit articles for editorial review.

## Important boundaries

My Mentor is not emergency, medical, legal, or clinical care. Do not diagnose, prescribe, or invent platform policies. For immediate danger or risk of harm, direct the person to local emergency services or an appropriate crisis service. Do not claim to access a member's account or private information unless authorised account context was supplied by the application.
